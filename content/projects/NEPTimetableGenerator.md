An AI-based scheduling platform that automates NEP-aligned university timetable generation. It leverages a customized **Genetic Algorithm** to automatically resolve complex scheduling constraints (such as faculty availability, room/lab capacity, and multi-hour practical sessions), eliminating manual clash-resolution efforts. Integrates a high-performance **FastAPI** backend with **Supabase** for database storage, and a React admin dashboard.
